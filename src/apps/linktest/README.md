# Link test

Temporary: it proves two Stickies can find each other over ESP-NOW, and it goes once Yahtzee plays between two devices.

## Using it

1. Open **LINK TEST** from the APPS page on both devices, and tap **FIND A PLAYER** on each.
2. They pair within a second or two. The headline then shows the other device's name, and a coin toss decides who goes first.
3. On your turn, **SEND +1** adds one to the counter and passes it over. On theirs, the button is grey.

Opening it turns Wi-Fi off, because the radio can't be on a network and in a match at once. The device doesn't sleep by itself while it's looking or playing. BACK, or holding OK to sleep, tells the other device straight away (`THEY LEFT`). Walking out of range shows `LOST THEM` after about 10 seconds.

Nothing is saved.
