# Link test

Temporary: it proves two Stickies can play over ESP-NOW, and it goes once Yahtzee plays between two devices.

## Using it

1. Open **LINK TEST** from the APPS page on both devices, and tap **FIND A PLAYER** on each. The match screen shows LOOKING FOR A PLAYER and two seats, with CANCEL under them.
2. They pair within a second or two, and a coin toss decides who goes first.
3. On your turn, **SEND +1** adds one to the counter and passes it over. On theirs, the button is grey.
4. Whoever reaches 10 ends the game. The final count stays up for a few seconds, then both see **PLAY AGAIN** and **LEAVE**. A new game starts once both say yes.

Opening a match turns Wi-Fi off, because the radio can't be on a network and in a match at once. The device doesn't sleep by itself while it's looking or a game is going on, but it can on the PLAY AGAIN screen. BACK leaves the match, and leaving or sleeping tells the other device straight away (`THEY LEFT`). Walking out of range shows `LOST CONNECTION` after about 10 seconds.

Nothing is saved.
